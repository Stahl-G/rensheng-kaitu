Kaitu for iPhone Privacy Policy

Updated October 18, 2026. Applies to the Kaitu (人生开图) iPhone app on the App Store, in the version without the Ask feature.

In short: Kaitu does not collect your data. The developer runs no servers and cannot see your notes, health data or schedule. Your data lives in two places only: this iPhone, and the storage location you choose.

Where your data is stored

  • This iPhone: a cache of the latest Today and Explore content, entries not yet sent, and app settings. Deleted together with the app.
  • The storage location you choose: an iCloud Drive folder, your own private GitHub repository, or another cloud folder you pick in Settings. Notes, confirmations and health data are written there and stored by that service (Apple, GitHub and so on) under its own terms. Kaitu does not handle them.
  • GitHub token: the token you enter is kept only in this iPhone's Keychain and is used only to read and write the repository you specify. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Health data

  • With your permission, Kaitu reads: steps, active energy, resting energy, workouts, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, body mass and body fat percentage. You can turn off each type in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • Kaitu only reads; it never writes to the Health app.
  • The data is converted to Kaitu's format and written to your chosen storage location (or to an address you enter yourself, such as your own Google Apps Script), so Kaitu on your computer can show trends and reminders.
  • Health data is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with any third party, and not used for medical diagnosis.

Other permissions

  • Calendar: when you confirm a plan on your phone, Kaitu adds it to a calendar named "开图" (Kaitu) and finds the same event later by Kaitu's own note. It does not read your other calendars' contents.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you hold to speak. The system's speech recognition turns speech into text, on device when possible; otherwise Apple processes it under Apple's privacy policy. Recordings are not kept.
  • Maps: the map on the Explore page is provided by Apple Maps, which requests map tiles from Apple. Places come from your own data at city level.

You can turn off any permission in Settings at any time. The related feature stops; everything else keeps working.

What Kaitu does not do

  • No accounts; nothing to sign up for or log into.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics or tracking code.
  • Does not send your data to AI services. This version has no AI features; if they are added, this page will be updated first and the app will ask for your consent before sending anything.
  • Crash reports: Apple shares anonymous crash information with the developer only if you chose "Share with App Developers" in system settings.

Children

Kaitu is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

  • Turn off any permission in system settings.
  • Disconnect the storage location in the app's settings; your data stays in your own iCloud or GitHub, for you to keep or delete.
  • Delete the app to remove all cache and settings from this iPhone.

Changes

This page is updated, with a new date above, when our practices change. New destinations for your data (such as AI features) will be explained in the app first.
