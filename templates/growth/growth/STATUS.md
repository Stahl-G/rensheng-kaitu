# 成长记录 / Growth journal

这个工作区只保存一个孩子的资料，由父母维护。先在「今天」快速记录一句原话，在「资料 → 收件箱」读回；「设置 → 导出全部资料」可带走当前工作区。

This workspace holds one child's notes, maintained by a parent. Capture an original observation in Today, read it in Library → Inbox, and use Settings → Export everything to take this workspace with you.

照片归档、成长时间线和小结核对入口尚未接入；不把孩子的成长照片录成体态照片。
Photo filing, the growth timeline and review approval are not connected yet. Do not file children's growth photos as body photos.
