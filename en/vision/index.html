Vision

Move one long-term goal forward, more smoothly.

You may know the feeling: an AI gave you plenty of good advice, and a few days later you cannot find it; you made a plan but cannot say how it is going; you write a little down every day and it never adds up to anything useful about yourself.

What Kaitu does

Kaitu keeps all of this in a place you own, so the AI you already use can pick up where you left off:

  • Tomorrow picks up from today. What was said, what got done and what did not — all findable. No reintroducing yourself, no tidying up after the AI.
  • Open it and the answer is there. The three things most worth doing today; what this project moved this week. No need to craft a prompt first.
  • What you record stays recorded. A note on your phone shows up on your computer; if syncing fails, it says so; an AI's suggestion is never filed as your decision.
  • It turns into insight. Scattered entries become something you can use: what stuck, where you keep getting stuck, where to go next.

Always yours

  • Your data lives where you choose: this computer, your iCloud, your own GitHub repository. Kaitu runs no servers and cannot see it.
  • Everything is visible and editable: memories and records are files you can open, change or delete, and roll back if you get it wrong.
  • Your calls stay yours: confirming a phase, starting a direction, writing to your calendar — an AI can only suggest; you say yes.
  • Switch AIs and your data stays. Use several later, and they read the same ledger.

What we will keep to

  • An open data format — not encrypted, not proprietary, yours to take away whole at any time.
  • Public, versioned rules for how an AI picks up the work, so any AI can join.
  • No ads, no selling data.
  • Open-source code.
