Vision

The one ledger all your agents share.

In 2026, always-on agents that act on your behalf arrived one after another: they read your mail, plan your days and keep working after you leave. You will likely use several at once, plus the ones on your computer and your phone. Each of them keeps notes on you — your goals, habits and what you are working on. But each keeps its own, they do not talk to each other, and most of it is locked inside each company's service: you cannot see all of it, and you cannot fix it.

Kaitu is that one ledger

Kaitu is not another agent. It is a folder you own: goals, next steps, schedule, records, memories and the road you have travelled, all in Markdown and JSON that people and agents can both read.

  • Yours. It lives where you choose: this computer, your iCloud, your own GitHub repository. Kaitu runs no servers and cannot see your data.
  • Open to any agent. Agents on your computer, always-on agents in the cloud and Ask on your phone read the same rules and hand off in the same format. Switch agents and the ledger stays.
  • Visible, editable, reversible. Every memory is a file you can open, edit or delete; every change is versioned and can be rolled back; who wrote what is on record.
  • Your calls stay yours. Decisions such as confirming a phase, starting a direction or writing to your calendar can only be suggested by an agent; you say yes.

The side made for you

The ledger is not only for agents. Kaitu draws it as something you glance at every day: the one thing that matters today, what this week added up to, where your life has got to. Closed, it runs today; open, it shows your life.

What we will keep to

  • An open data format — not encrypted, not proprietary, yours to take away whole at any time.
  • Public, versioned rules for how agents pick up the work, so any agent can join.
  • No ads, no selling data.
  • Open-source code.

KaituLife, coming into focus.
