iPhone FAQ

Why is the app empty?

The phone shows what Kaitu on your computer generates. Open Kaitu on your computer, choose the same data location as the phone (an iCloud folder or your own GitHub repository), and today's view appears once it is generated.

Where do my notes go?

They are saved on the phone first and sent to your data location when you are online; once your computer processes them they show as "Updated". Capturing works offline.

Where does my health data go?

Only to the data location you chose. Kaitu runs no servers and cannot see your data. See the iPhone privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app clears its cache on the phone; the files in your data location are yours to manage and can be deleted directly.
