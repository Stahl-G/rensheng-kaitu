iPhone FAQ

Do I need a computer or cloud storage?

No. Start with a local profile, notes and tasks on the phone and continue after reopening. For an existing connected workspace, the app reads its phone snapshots. If snapshots are missing, ask your agent to “update Kaitu” or generate phone content on a computer using the same workspace. The phone does not invent a second set of remote snapshots.

Where do my notes go?

Explicit saves in a phone-created workspace enter its local files. External workspaces use inbox delivery: saved locally, delivered to a folder and a real processing receipt are separate states. File delivery does not mean a computer has processed it.

What does Ask require?

Your own AI provider key; charges and quotas belong to that provider account. Related context is sent with a question, as described in the iPhone privacy policy. Without a key you can still keep local notes and review and export “About me for AI” to your usual AI.

Why might Google Drive authorization be unavailable?

The initial public beta focuses on local features; Drive is restricted testing. If the Google project is still in Testing, your Google account must be registered as a test user. Being signed in is not the same as granting Drive access or connecting a folder. Your existing location remains until a new connection succeeds.

Where does health data go?

Onboarding health inputs stay separately on the phone and are excluded from default sharing. The separate health-sync feature delivers data to your configured location or receiver. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Export anything you want to keep, then remove keys or disconnect in the app. Revoke remote grants in the service and manage its files there. Uninstalling does not delete cloud files or system backups, or revoke grants.

How do I report a problem?

Include the app version, steps and actual result. Hide keys, private text and health data in screenshots. Send private information by the email above, not in a public issue.
