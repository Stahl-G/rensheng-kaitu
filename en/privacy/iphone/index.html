Kaitu for iPhone Privacy Policy

For Kaitu on iPhone, including TestFlight beta builds.

Updated October 22, 2026. Applies to Kaitu for iPhone 0.7.2, including Ask in TestFlight builds.

The developer does not run a Kaitu cloud server that receives your personal workspace. You can use the app on your phone alone. Cloud storage, AI, speech recognition and online maps send necessary data to the services you choose or use.

Files and credentials

  • On your phone: Local profiles, notes, tasks, unsent text drafts, conversation history, workspace caches and items waiting to be delivered are stored in the app.
  • Your chosen location: Connected folders, iCloud, private GitHub repositories and Google Drive store the relevant files under their own services. Saving locally, delivering a file and receiving a processing receipt are separate states.
  • Credentials: AI keys and GitHub credentials are stored in the device Keychain. Google Sign-In manages Google login. Credentials are used for your chosen services and are not sent to the Kaitu developer.

Ask and context shared with AI

Ask is optional and requires your own provider key. Charges and quotas belong to that provider account. Current options are DeepSeek, Kimi, Zhipu and OpenAI.

When you send a question, your question, workspace rules, context summaries, retrieved file excerpts and recent conversation are sent to the selected AI service. Images that you explicitly select and send go to models that support images. The provider processes requests under its own privacy policy.

You review and confirm “About me for AI” before copying or exporting it. Sensitive items are excluded by default, and changed selections require confirmation again. Model suggestions become saved information only after you explicitly accept them.

Photos, camera and speech

  • Photo footprints: With system permission, the scan reads the capture dates and location metadata of accessible photos, not their pixels. It saves city centers and inferred experiences, not original precise GPS or photo asset identifiers. Inferred experiences still need your review.
  • Camera and image attachments: Images are accessed only when you explicitly capture or select them. Attaching a file is separate from sending an image to a model.
  • Speech: Apple system speech recognition transcribes your speech, on device when supported; otherwise Apple’s recognition service may process it. You can edit the transcript before explicitly saving it. The app does not retain audio recordings.

Health and calendar

You choose Health permissions in the system prompt. Kaitu reads but does not write Apple Health data. The requested types depend on whether you use onboarding or the health-sync feature.

Raw health inputs collected during onboarding are stored separately on the phone and excluded from default context sharing. If you separately enable health sync, permitted raw samples are delivered to your selected location or receiver, such as your own Google Apps Script. The developer does not receive these samples, use them for advertising or sell them.

Kaitu event records and Apple Calendar are separate destinations. Writing to the “Kaitu” calendar requires the calendar integration and system permission. A local save does not mean the event has synced to the system or a computer.

GitHub and Google Drive

GitHub login or a token accesses only an authorized workspace repository. Google Drive requests the full Drive scope: Google grants read/write access across Drive, while the app restricts workspace operations to your selected Kaitu folder.

Google Drive is a restricted feature in the initial public beta. If the Google project is still in Testing, only registered test accounts can authorize it. A TestFlight invitation does not grant Google authorization eligibility.

Disconnecting locally does not delete remote files or revoke a remote grant. Manage grants in Google account connections or GitHub authorized apps.

Maps, diagnostics and feedback

Maps use MapLibre and may request styles or tiles from OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap-related services and Terrain elevation services. These services receive ordinary request information, including your IP address and the requested map area. Footprints use cities from your workspace, not original precise photo GPS.

The app has no advertising and does not integrate third-party behavioral analytics or advertising-tracking SDKs. TestFlight feedback and Apple-provided crash diagnostics are subject to Apple’s terms. If you email us or submit an issue, the developer receives what you send. Do not include keys, private conversations or raw health data in public feedback.

Your controls and deletion

  • Local profiles and notes do not require a Kaitu account. AI, cloud services and system permissions are optional for their respective features.
  • Disable Photos, Health, camera, microphone, speech or calendar permissions in system settings to stop the corresponding access.
  • Delete AI keys, sign out of third-party services or disconnect the workspace in the app. Revoke remote grants in the service’s account settings when needed.
  • Uninstalling removes app files but does not delete cloud copies, system backups or revoke third-party grants. Manage cloud files in your chosen service.

Children and updates

Kaitu is not directed at children under 13. We update this page and its date when data practices change, and describe new destinations in the relevant feature.
